What Is USD Bloom?

USD Bloom is a yield-bearing stablecoin designed to grow capital while staying pegged to the US dollar. It brings stability, composability, and passive yield together in one asset.

Capital That Grows

Earn passive yield as your stablecoins are put to work in DeFi protocols.

Always Liquid, Always Stable

Stay pegged to the US dollar and access your funds without lockups or unnecessary delays.

Fully Automatic

USD Bloom works in the background, so users and teams can focus on their products without managing strategies manually.
